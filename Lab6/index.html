Kurnool is a city where the past remains visible in the landscape.

Located in Andhra Pradesh, Kurnool brings together historic landmarks, dramatic rock formations, ancient caves and spiritual destinations.

It is a place that rewards curiosity — every road leading somewhere with a story.

Rising from the centre of Kurnool, Konda Reddy Buruju is one of the city's most recognisable historic landmarks.

Its enduring structure offers a glimpse into the architectural and historical character of the region.
